📌 Idea / Concept: Proving Similarity of Two Polygons & Finding the Similarity Ratio

📌 Proving Similarity of Two Polygons
1 In the given figure: Prove that the two polygons $ABCD$ and $EFGH$ are similar and write the similarity ratio of polygon $ABCD$ to polygon $EFGH$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 1
  • In the two polygons $ABCD$ and $EFGH$:
  • 1) Corresponding angles are congruent:
    $m(\angle A) = m(\angle E)$, $m(\angle B) = m(\angle F)$, $m(\angle C) = m(\angle G) = 90^\circ$, and $m(\angle D) = m(\angle H)$.
  • 2) Corresponding side lengths are proportional:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{EF} = \dfrac{12}{8} = \dfrac{3}{2}, \quad \dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}, \quad \dfrac{CD}{GH} = \dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}, \quad \dfrac{DA}{HE} = \dfrac{15}{10} = \dfrac{3}{2}$$
  • Since $\dfrac{AB}{EF} = \dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{CD}{GH} = \dfrac{DA}{HE} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and corresponding angles are equal:
Polygon $ABCD \sim$ Polygon $EFGH$  |  Similarity Ratio $= \dfrac{3}{2}$

✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 2
  • In the two polygons $ABCD$ and $HLKJ$:
  • 1) Corresponding angles are congruent:

Polygon $ABCD \sim$ Polygon $HLKJ$  |  Similarity Ratio $= \dfrac{1}{2}$

📌 Idea / Concept: Finding Missing Angles & Side Lengths in Similar Polygons

📌 Using Similarity to Find Unknown Sides & Angles
3 In the given figure: If polygon $ABCD \sim$ polygon $EFGH$:
1Find: $m(\angle C)$, $m(\angle H)$
2Find the length of each of $\overline{FG}$ and $\overline{DC}$
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 3
  • 1) Finding $m(\angle C)$ and $m(\angle H)$:
    Since polygon $ABCD \sim$ polygon $EFGH$:
    $m(\angle A) = m(\angle E) = 90^\circ$,   $m(\angle B) = m(\angle F) = 120^\circ$,   and   $m(\angle C) = m(\angle G) = 45^\circ$.
    Since the sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral is $360^\circ$:
    $$m(\angle D) = m(\angle H) = 360^\circ - (90^\circ + 120^\circ + 45^\circ) = 105^\circ$$
  • 2) Finding the lengths of $\overline{FG}$ and $\overline{DC}$:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{EF} = \dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{DC}{HG} \implies \dfrac{15\sqrt{3}}{5\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{30}{FG} = \dfrac{DC}{10\sqrt{2}}$$ $$FG = \dfrac{5\sqrt{3} \times 30}{15\sqrt{3}} = 10\text{ cm}, \qquad DC = \dfrac{15\sqrt{3} \times 10\sqrt{2}}{5\sqrt{3}} = 30\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$$
$m(\angle C) = 45^\circ, \ m(\angle H) = 105^\circ, \ FG = 10\text{ cm}, \ DC = 30\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$
4 Self-Evaluation In the following figure: If $ABCD \sim EFGH$:
1Find: $m(\angle C)$, $m(\angle D)$
2Find the length of each of $\overline{FG}$, $\overline{AD}$
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 4
  • 1) Finding $m(\angle C)$ and $m(\angle D)$:
    Since $ABCD \sim EFGH$:   $m(\angle C) = m(\angle G) = 135^\circ$.
    In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $m(\angle A) = 75^\circ$ and $m(\angle B) = 90^\circ$:
    $$m(\angle D) = 360^\circ - (75^\circ + 90^\circ + 135^\circ) = 60^\circ$$
  • 2) Finding the lengths of $\overline{FG}$ and $\overline{AD}$:
    $$\dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{CD}{GH} = \dfrac{AD}{EH} \implies \dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{FG} = \dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{8\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{AD}{16\sqrt{2}}$$ Since the similarity ratio is $\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{8\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$:
    $$FG = 2 \times 4\sqrt{3} = 8\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}, \qquad AD = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 16\sqrt{2} = 8\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$$
$m(\angle C) = 135^\circ, \ m(\angle D) = 60^\circ, \ FG = 8\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}, \ AD = 8\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$

📌 Idea / Concept: Proving Similarity in Rectangles, Rhombuses, Squares & Parallelograms

📌 Similarity of Rectangles & Rhombuses
5 In each of the following, prove that the two polygons are similar, and write the similarity ratio:
1Rectangle $ABCD$ to rectangle $XYZM$
2Rhombus $ABCD$ to rhombus $EFGH$
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 5
  • 1) Rectangles $ABCD$ and $XYZM$:
    All interior angles of both rectangles are $90^\circ$, and:
    $$\dfrac{BC}{YZ} = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2, \quad \dfrac{CD}{ZM} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2 \implies \dfrac{BC}{YZ} = \dfrac{CD}{ZM} = 2$$ Therefore, $\text{Rectangle } ABCD \sim \text{Rectangle } XYZM$ with similarity ratio $= 2$.
  • 2) Rhombuses $ABCD$ and $EFGH$:
    In rhombus $ABCD$: $m(\angle C) = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ = m(\angle G)$. Thus all corresponding angles are equal ($110^\circ$ and $70^\circ$).
    Since all sides of a rhombus are equal in length, the ratio between any two corresponding sides is:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{EF} = \dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{CD}{GH} = \dfrac{DA}{HE} = \dfrac{3}{5}$$ Therefore, $\text{Rhombus } ABCD \sim \text{Rhombus } EFGH$ with similarity ratio $= \dfrac{3}{5}$.
(1) Similarity Ratio $= 2$  |  (2) Similarity Ratio $= \dfrac{3}{5}$
6 Self-Evaluation In each of the following, prove that the two polygons are similar, and write the similarity ratio:
1Square $ABCD$ to square $LMNO$
2Parallelogram $ABCD$ to parallelogram $PQRS$
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 6
  • 1) Square $ABCD$ and Square $LMNO$:
    All angles of both squares are $90^\circ$, and all sides of each square are equal:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{LM} = \dfrac{BC}{MN} = \dfrac{CD}{NO} = \dfrac{DA}{OL} = \dfrac{15}{6} = \dfrac{5}{2}$$ Therefore, $\text{Square } ABCD \sim \text{Square } LMNO$ with similarity ratio $= \dfrac{5}{2}$.
  • 2) Parallelogram $ABCD$ and Parallelogram $PQRS$:
    In parallelogram $ABCD$: $m(\angle A) = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ = m(\angle P)$, so all corresponding angles are equal ($60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$).
    Ratio of corresponding sides:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{8}{6} = \dfrac{4}{3}, \qquad \dfrac{AD}{PS} = \dfrac{6}{4.5} = \dfrac{4}{3}$$ Therefore, $\text{Parallelogram } ABCD \sim \text{Parallelogram } PQRS$ with similarity ratio $= \dfrac{4}{3}$.
(1) Similarity Ratio $= \dfrac{5}{2}$  |  (2) Similarity Ratio $= \dfrac{4}{3}$

📌 Idea / Concept: Ratio Between Perimeters of Two Similar Polygons & Algebraic Unknowns

📌 Ratio of Perimeters = Ratio of Corresponding Sides
7 In the given figure: If polygon $ABCD \sim$ polygon $EFGH$, and $\dfrac{\text{Perimeter of polygon } ABCD}{\text{Perimeter of polygon } EFGH} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, find the value of each of: $x, y, z$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 7
  • Since polygon $ABCD \sim$ polygon $EFGH$ and $\dfrac{\text{Perimeter of } ABCD}{\text{Perimeter of } EFGH} = \dfrac{3}{2}$:
  • 1) Finding $z$:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{EF} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies \dfrac{6}{z} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies z = \dfrac{2 \times 6}{3} = 4$$
  • 2) Finding $y$:
    $$\dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies \dfrac{y + 3}{5} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies y + 3 = 7.5 \implies y = 7.5 - 3 = 4.5$$
  • 3) Finding $x$:
    $$\dfrac{CD}{GH} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies \dfrac{2x + 1}{x + 2} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies 4x + 2 = 3x + 6 \implies x = 4$$
$x = 4, \quad y = 4.5, \quad z = 4$
8 Self-Evaluation In the following figure: If $ABCD \sim EFGH$, and $\dfrac{\text{Perimeter of } ABCD}{\text{Perimeter of } EFGH} = \dfrac{3}{4}$, find the value of each of $x, y, z$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 8
  • Since $ABCD \sim EFGH$ and $\dfrac{\text{Perimeter of } ABCD}{\text{Perimeter of } EFGH} = \dfrac{3}{4}$:
  • 1) Finding $z$:
    $$\dfrac{AB}{EF} = \dfrac{3}{4} \implies \dfrac{2z - 3}{8} = \dfrac{3}{4} \implies 2z - 3 = 6 \implies 2z = 9 \implies z = 4.5$$
  • 2) Finding $x$:
    $$\dfrac{BC}{FG} = \dfrac{3}{4} \implies \dfrac{x + 1}{2x} = \dfrac{3}{4} \implies 6x = 4x + 4 \implies 2x = 4 \implies x = 2$$
  • 3) Finding $y$:
    $$\dfrac{CD}{GH} = \dfrac{3}{4} \implies \dfrac{4.5}{2y} = \dfrac{3}{4} \implies 6y = 18 \implies y = 3$$
$x = 2, \quad y = 3, \quad z = 4.5$

14 Choose the correct answer from the given ones: In the following figure:

📌 Word Problem on Ratio Between Perimeters
15 Choose the correct answer from the given ones:
Two similar polygons, the ratio between the lengths of two corresponding sides is $2 : 3$, and the perimeter of the smaller polygon is $34\text{ cm}$. What is the perimeter of the larger polygon?
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 15
  • The ratio between the perimeters of two similar polygons equals the ratio between the lengths of any two corresponding sides:
  • $$\dfrac{\text{Perimeter of smaller polygon}}{\text{Perimeter of larger polygon}} = \dfrac{2}{3}$$
  • $$\dfrac{34}{\text{Perimeter of larger polygon}} = \dfrac{2}{3} \implies \text{Perimeter of larger polygon} = \dfrac{3 \times 34}{2} = 51\text{ cm}$$
Correct Choice: (c) $51\text{ cm}$
